What we covered

TrainingOne-time process of adjusting model weights by predicting next tokens across trillions of examples.
WeightsFrozen numerical parameters that encode the model's knowledge after training finishes.
InferenceServing the model at runtime: repeat a full token step (network forward pass to logits, then softmax and sampling, then append) until the response is complete.
Forward passThe deterministic stack from raw text through tokenization, embedding, and transformer layers to logits — one raw score per vocabulary token, before softmax.
TokenizationSplitting raw text into subword tokens and mapping each to an integer ID — the very first stage of every forward pass.
EmbeddingConverting token IDs into dense vectors so the transformer can do mathematical operations on meaning.
TransformerThe N-layer attention-based network that processes the embedded vectors and builds a contextual representation of the full prompt.
LogitsRaw, unnormalized scores the model assigns to every vocabulary token before softmax is applied. Higher logit = higher probability after normalization.
TemperatureParameter that controls how flat or spiky the token probability distribution is at sampling time.
