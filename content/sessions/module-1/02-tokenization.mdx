<SessionSection id="big-picture" label="Big Picture">

## Tokenization sits between raw text and model input

The tokenizer is the bridge between human-readable text and the discrete symbols the model can process.

</SessionSection>

<SessionSection id="core-concepts" label="Core Concepts">

## Why token boundaries matter

Tokenization affects prompt length, model pricing, and how efficiently the model can represent text.

</SessionSection>

<SessionSection id="practical-example" label="Practical Example">

## Counting tokens in a real prompt

A short sentence may map to far more model tokens than you expect, especially with punctuation, code, or mixed languages.

</SessionSection>
