## Why this roadmap

You can do a lot with just ChatGPT or Claude: build websites and apps, edit videos, and speed up your daily work.

The companies that make them both run free academies:

- [OpenAI Academy](https://academy.openai.com/): courses for ChatGPT and Codex.
- [Claude Academy](https://academy.claude.com/): courses, tutorials, and use cases for Claude and Claude Code.

You don't need both. Pick the tool you already use and follow the three steps below.

## Step 1: Learn the basics

### If you use ChatGPT

Open [OpenAI Academy courses](https://academy.openai.com/pages/courses?linkMenu=Courses) and go to **Apply AI at Work**. Pick your level:

<GuideTable
  columns={["Your level", "Course", "What you learn"]}
  rows={[
    ["New to AI", "AI Foundations", "The basics of AI and ChatGPT: clear instructions, useful context, checking answers"],
    ["Some experience", "Applied AI Foundations", "Turn a task you repeat into a step-by-step workflow"],
    ["Comfortable with AI", "Agents and Workflows", "Guide AI agents through structured work and reuse what works"],
  ]}
/>

### If you use Claude

Open [Claude courses](https://academy.claude.com/products/claude/courses):

1. Start with **Claude 101**: your first chat, good prompts, projects, artifacts, and skills.
2. Then take **AI Fluency: Framework and foundations** to learn how to work with AI well and safely.
3. Check back often. New courses are added as Claude gets new features.

## Step 3: Learn by doing (my favorite)

Courses are good. Trying real examples is better. When you see what other people build, you get ideas for your own work.

- [Claude tutorials](https://academy.claude.com/products/claude/tutorials): short how-to lessons.
- [Claude use cases](https://academy.claude.com/products/claude/use-cases): real tasks you can try in Claude.
- [All use cases](https://academy.claude.com/use-cases): filter by your job, like Marketing, HR, Finance, Engineering, or Design.

### How to use a use case

1. Pick one that is close to a task you do every week.
2. Follow it once, step by step.
3. Do it again with your own real work.
4. Keep the prompt that worked, so next time is faster.

## Tips before you start

- **One tool first.** Finish Step 1 with one tool before you try the other.
- **Apply it the same week.** After each course, use what you learned on one real task.
- **Courses are free, tools may not be.** Some features, like coding agents, can need a paid plan. Check the pricing page before you start.
