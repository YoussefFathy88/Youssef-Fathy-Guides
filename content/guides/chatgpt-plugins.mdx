## The short version

1. Open **ChatGPT**.
2. Click **Plugins** in the left sidebar.
3. **Search** for the app you want.
4. Click **+** and **connect** your account.
5. In a new chat, type **@** and the plugin's name, then your request.

The same steps work for any app in the Plugins list. Below are the three apps from the video.

## Step 1: Open Plugins in ChatGPT

- Open ChatGPT and look at the left sidebar.
- Click **Plugins**. The page says "Connect plugins to let ChatGPT work across your tools".

## Step 2: Search for the app and connect it

- Type the app's name in the search box at the top right.
- Click **+** next to the right app. Check the name under it, because similar names show up too.
- Sign in to that app and allow access when it asks. Once it is connected, the **+** turns into **⋯**.

## Step 3: Use @ so ChatGPT works with the plugin

This is the step most people miss. Connecting a plugin is not enough. **Start your message with @ and the plugin's name.** This tells ChatGPT exactly which plugin to use. Without it, ChatGPT may answer from its own knowledge and never open the app.

- Type **@** in the message box, then the first letters of the name.
- Pick the plugin from the list that pops up.
- Write your request after it.

### Example messages

- `@Tarteel` explain the meaning of Surah Al-Kahf, verse 10
- `@Buffer` schedule this post for tomorrow at 6 pm
- `@MyFitnessPal` make me a high-protein meal plan for today

## If it doesn't work

- **The plugin isn't used:** start a new chat and put the @ mention at the start of the message.
- **It asks you to sign in again:** open Plugins, click **⋯** next to the plugin, and connect it again.
- **You can't find an app:** check the spelling, or search a shorter part of its name ("my fitness" instead of "myfitnesspal").
